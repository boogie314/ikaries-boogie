ABOUT ME

The person behind the name.

IKARIES BOOGIE

Ikaries Kiplangat Derrick, known by many as Ikaries Boogie, is a 21-year-old from Taboino who is passionate about technology, software engineering and personal growth.

I was born and raised in Taboino, a humble place that has played a big part in shaping who I am today. Growing up here has taught me a lot about life and made me understand that where you come from does not have to decide where you are going.

I'm 21 years old and honestly, I'm still learning and figuring out life step by step. I don't have everything figured out yet, but I have dreams and I'm trying my best to work towards them.

I'm really passionate about technology and software engineering. I enjoy learning about tech and seeing what can be done with it. I believe it can change my life, and hopefully one day I'll be able to build things that can also help other people.

I've also had the chance to experience life outside my home environment, meet different people, learn new things and of course have some good vibes with my friends. 😂 Those experiences have opened my mind and made me realize there is a lot more to life than what I already know.

As a person, I would say I'm ambitious, hardworking, curious, friendly and sometimes playful 😂. I enjoy good conversations, good company and just being around people I'm comfortable with.

I also value honesty. I'd rather be myself and be honest about who I am than try to act like someone I'm not.

Of course I'm not perfect. I make mistakes, I have weaknesses and there are still many things about myself that I'm trying to improve. But I believe that's part of growing up and becoming a better person.

My biggest goal is to become a better person, build something I can be proud of and most importantly, make my mum proud.

Taboino is where I started, and I'm proud of where I come from. But I also believe there is much more ahead of me.

I'm still figuring things out, but I know I'm going somewhere. I'm just getting started. ❤️
